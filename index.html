Rohde Ecotop 60 — Corner Thermal & Airflow Simulation

This repository models the steady-state radiative, convective, conductive, and buoyant-plume heat transfer of a Rohde Ecotop 60 pottery kiln installed in a masonry studio corner. It evaluates wall standoffs, radiation shields, floor insulation pads, and exhaust hood ventilation across 3,456 parameter combinations to ensure surrounding building materials remain within safe temperature limits.

  • Interactive Corner Thermal Explorer (`kiln_explorer.html`)
    Interactive web visualization of all 3,456 simulated configurations with top-down plan and side-elevation views of temperatures and airflow.
  • Thermal & Plume Physics Model (`thermal_maths.pdf`)
    Mathematical formulation of the coupled radiative view-factor network, 1D conductive stacks, natural convection correlations, and buoyant exhaust plume equations.
  • Software Architecture & Implementation Plan (`software_implementation.pdf`)
    Design specification detailing the Protocol Buffer schemas, surface mesh representation, numerical solvers, and Bazel build structure.
